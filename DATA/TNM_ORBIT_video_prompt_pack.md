## VERSION A — Master (30 seconds, one continuous shot)

Full 30-second arc: macro dew-drop open → pull-back + 360° orbit → broccoli / avocado / tomato
macro passes → heart-halo hero settle → drift back to the opening macro frame (loop).

```text
SCENE CONTEXT
A premium silent product film for a health book. A hardcover book levitates in a seamless cream-white infinity studio while fresh ingredients orbit it like planets. The camera performs one slow unbroken 360-degree orbit around the book across 30 seconds, passing through macro moments of the orbiting ingredients. Warm, clean, minimal keynote mood.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@image1 = the book: hardcover with cream-white cover, dark forest green lowercase title "the new me 2.0", a heart-shaped emblem built from vegetables (spinach leaves, broccoli, avocado half, cherry tomato, green peas), white subtitle band "REVERSE AGING", author name "GAGAN DHAWAN" at the bottom, three small dots (green, yellow, green) bottom right. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: orbiting ingredients drifting past the camera at different depths — crisp spinach leaves, one broccoli floret, one avocado half with brown pit, one glossy cherry tomato, six round green peas, all beaded with fine dew droplets.
Midground: the book levitating 30 cm above a seamless cream studio floor, its soft contact shadow pooled beneath it.
Background: an infinite cream cyclorama wall with gentle brightness falloff; a soft green heart-shaped shadow casts onto the wall when the backlight sits behind the book.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Frame opens on an extreme macro: a spinach leaf fills the frame, a 4 mm dew drop on its surface refracting the studio light; the book sits far behind as a soft green blur. Camera is 40 cm from the leaf at 1.1 m height, dead level.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot lasting 30 seconds. The camera does not cut on its own.

OPTICS
12° tele-detail for the opening macro; the camera settles to 47° neutral FOV once at orbit distance and holds 47° for the rest of the take. Shallow depth of field during macro moments, deeper focus at orbit distance. No drift mid-take.

CAMERA
Smooth circular dolly move: starts 40 cm from the spinach leaf, pulls back and rises to 1.1 m height at a 1.2 m orbit radius, then orbits the levitating book at a steady 1 km/h, completing one full circle in about 24 seconds and continuing past the hero angle toward its starting side. Focus starts racked on the dew drop, transfers to the book during the pull-back, racks to macro twice more during the orbit, and returns to the dew drop at the end. Camera stays level, never tilting past 5 degrees.

ACTION
0.0s to 5.0s — macro hold on the dew drop trembling with surface tension; the leaf drifts slowly left; behind it the book sharpens from blur into view. Small dark-green geometric sans-serif text fades in beside the leaf: "The guidebook for the new you."
5.0s to 12.0s — camera pulls back and begins the orbit; ingredients enter the frame one by one, drifting counterclockwise around the book at their own radii, each slowly tumbling; a soft highlight band sweeps across the book cover from left to right. Text fades out at 10.0s.
12.0s to 16.0s — the broccoli floret passes close to camera; focus racks from the book to the floret's fractal detail with glinting dew beads, then racks back to the book. Text fades in at 13.0s in the upper third: "Eat better. Move more."
16.0s to 21.0s — the avocado half crosses the frame, its pit catching a warm glint; the cherry tomato follows with dew beading and rolling on its skin. Text changes at 17.0s to "Age in reverse." and fades out at 20.0s.
21.0s to 26.0s — the orbit decelerates smoothly into the hero three-quarter angle; the ingredients slow and align into a heart-shaped halo behind the book, echoing the cover emblem; the soft green heart-shaped shadow blooms on the cyclorama wall; the cover turns a few degrees toward dead-on and every line of cover text stays legible.
26.0s to 30.0s — the camera keeps moving past the hero angle and eases back in toward the floating spinach leaf, focus racking back to the trembling dew drop, the book softening to a green blur behind — the final frame matches the opening frame.

PHYSICS
Weightless drift: ingredients float at 0.2 to 0.5 km/h, each tumbling slowly around its own axis, one full rotation per 6 seconds. Dew drops hold spherical surface tension and slide only when a leaf tilts. The book hangs nearly still with a faint 1 cm vertical bob, its contact shadow breathing in sync on the floor. Peas bob gently in place.

LIGHTING
One large soft source high camera-left at 5600K neutral daylight wrapping the cream set, with warm bounce from the floor lifting the shadows. A dimmer warm backlight behind the book builds the heart shadow on the wall. Exposure is bright and airy with gentle highlight roll-off; the cover stays evenly lit and legible at every angle.

COLOR GRADE
Warm cream and bone neutrals carry the set; the only saturated color comes from the ingredient greens and the tomato red, kept rich and true so the cover art reads exactly. Matte highlights, no crushed blacks.

STYLE
Photoreal premium product film, 8K detail intent, fine film grain, clean minimal keynote mood.

OUTPUT SETTINGS
16:9 landscape, real-time speed throughout, 30 seconds total.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous shot for the full 30 seconds; the camera does not cut on its own.
The cover artwork matches @image1 exactly at every angle: title "the new me 2.0", the white band "REVERSE AGING", "GAGAN DHAWAN", the vegetable heart emblem, the three dots.
The book is a real physical hardcover with consistent thickness, spine and page edges.
Ingredients stay real food: crisp spinach, one broccoli floret, one avocado half with brown pit, one round cherry tomato, six round peas; food never morphs or melts.
On-screen text renders exactly as written, dark forest green geometric sans-serif, fully legible while on screen.
Orbit is smooth and constant-rate with no jump cuts and no speed ramps.
The final frame matches the first frame for a seamless loop.
```
